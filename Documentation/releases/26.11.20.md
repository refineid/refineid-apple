## What this candidate carries

- The trusted-list fetch and the raced trusted-list lookup stay inside their
  task-group scopes, so signing a document no longer faults at the
  timestamping step on macOS.
- A cancelled list worker stops instead of being recorded as a failed fetch,
  and no plain attempt follows a cancellation.
- The process-events helper reports a process it never watched as untracked
  rather than as exited.
